# Screens for scripts/visual-check.sh, one per line:
#   name | allowed change | launch arguments
# The allowed change is the share of pixels that may differ (blank: the default, 0.3%). Screens
# with motion that never settles (the analog second hand, the cycling phone on the first
# onboarding page) get more. Every screen is taken in Paper, Graphite and Mono; lines starting
# with "landscape" also run turned. -GPDemo, -GPTheme and a fixed clock are added by the script.
