Describe it

In one line

Glasspad turns your iPhone into a trackpad, keyboard, remote and button deck for your Mac. Free and open source.

In a paragraph

Glasspad is a free, open source (MIT) app that turns an iPhone into a trackpad, keyboard, media remote and button deck for a Mac. It works as a trackpad with a tap you can feel, a keyboard, a remote for Netflix and YouTube, a clicker for slides, and buttons that follow the app in front. An iPhone app and a small Mac menu bar app talk over your Wi-Fi, paired once by QR code and encrypted, with no account. It is made by Soham Aggarwal.
