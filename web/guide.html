Guide

Everything Glasspad does, a few lines each.

Get started

  1. Install. Paste this into Terminal on your Mac. It walks you through Xcode, your Apple ID and the iPhone, and waits for you where Apple needs a click.
    curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Soham109/glasspad/main/install.sh | bash
    Every step, with pictures: Install.
  2. Allow Accessibility. When System Settings opens, switch Glasspad on so it can move the pointer.
  3. Pair. Click Glasspad in the menu bar, choose Pair Your iPhone, then scan the code with the Glasspad app or the Camera.

Switching modes

  • Tap the mode button at the top of the screen for the mode menu.
  • Clipboard, Present, Remote and Displays open full screen from the same menu.
  • Settings is there too: Appearance, Trackpad, Haptics, the modes you show, Advanced and About.

Modes

Trackpad

  • One finger moves the pointer. Tap to click, and feel it.
  • Hold a finger still for a moment to force click.
  • Two fingers scroll, pinch to zoom, or tap for a right click.
  • Three fingers swipe between Spaces, or up for Mission Control.

Keyboard

  • Type or dictate. The text lands wherever the cursor is on the Mac.
  • control, option, command and shift, the usual shortcuts, arrows, and fn for F1 to F12.

Media

  • What is playing in Spotify, Music, Podcasts or a browser.
  • Drag to scrub. Set the volume, the sound output and the screen brightness.

Dock

  • Your Mac's Dock. Tap an app to open it or switch to it.
  • Touch and hold an app to hide or quit it.
  • The ··· menu hides the Mac's own Dock, always or only while connected.

Actions

  • The top follows the app in front on your Mac. More than 70 apps have their own buttons.
  • Pinned holds yours. See Make Actions yours.

Remote

  • Tap the pad to play or pause. Swipe sideways to skip 10 seconds, up or down for volume.
  • The buttons follow the player: Skip Intro on Netflix, Subtitles and Next on YouTube.
  • Search types on the Mac and presses Return. The screen stays dark and dim.

Present

  • Large Next and Back buttons, and a timer.
  • Touch and hold the pad to point with a laser on the slide.

Clipboard

  • Send to Mac: tap Paste to send what you copied on the iPhone, text or an image.
  • Get from Mac: tap it to copy what your Mac has copied.

Displays

  • Your displays as in System Settings, with every window on them.
  • Tap a window to bring it forward, or drag it to another display.
  • Touch and hold a window for the halves, Fill, Center and Split With.
  • The settings button sets brightness, resolution, Night Shift and the main display.

Layouts

  • In the mode menu, tap + next to Layouts and start from a template.
  • Up to three panes. Tap a pane to change its mode.
  • Drag the line to resize; it rests on thirds and the middle. Double tap it to even out, or drag it off the edge to remove a pane.
  • Your layouts sit in the mode menu, next to the modes.

StandBy

  • Pick the StandBy layout, or Clock and Trackpad, and stand the iPhone up while it charges.
  • Tap the clock to change its face: digital, analog or minimal. Now Playing and Calendar can sit beside it.
  • At night everything turns a dim red.

Make Actions yours

Library

  • Tap + in Pinned. Every app, Mac control, macro, shortcut and website is here.
  • Search, then tap + to pin. Tap the check to unpin.

Any button

  • Touch and hold a button to change its name, symbol, color and size.
  • Add a website and its logo appears by itself.

Folders

  • Add a Folder, save it, then tap it and add buttons inside.
  • Edit, next to the app's name, changes the buttons for that app.

Macros

  • In the Library, tap Record New Macro and do the steps on your Mac.
  • The macro becomes a button in Pinned. Tap it to replay the steps.

Themes

  • Settings, then Appearance.
  • Finger Dots, Mode Button, Start In and Left-Handed are set there too.

Help

The pointer does not move
System Settings › Privacy & Security › Accessibility: switch Glasspad off and on. Reinstalls can reset it.
The iPhone cannot find the Mac
Put both on the same Wi-Fi, and allow Local Network in Settings › Privacy & Security › Local Network › Glasspad.
Media shows no song from Spotify or Music
System Settings › Privacy & Security › Automation › Glasspad: switch on Spotify and Music.
The app stopped opening after a week
With a free Apple ID, your Mac renews the app over Wi-Fi before the 7 days are up. It could not reach the phone in time: bring the phone onto the Mac's Wi-Fi and run ~/Projects/glasspad/scripts/refresh-phone.sh --now.
Is anything sent over the internet?
No. Glasspad pairs once with a QR code, encrypts everything, and your iPhone and Mac talk only over your Wi-Fi. There is no account. Website buttons fetch their site's name and logo from that site.
