Privacy

Privacy Policy

Short version: Glasspad collects nothing about you. Last updated 17 October 2026.

The apps

Glasspad has an iPhone app and a Mac app. They talk to each other directly over your Wi-Fi. There is no Glasspad server, no account and no sign-in.

  • No data collection. The apps have no analytics, no tracking, no advertising and no crash reporting service. Nothing about you or how you use them is sent to me or to anyone else.
  • Pairing and encryption. You pair the iPhone and the Mac once with a QR code. Everything they send each other is encrypted with a key that stays on your two devices.
  • What stays on your devices. Your settings, layouts, buttons, macros and clipboard history are stored only on your iPhone and your Mac.

The only requests that leave your Wi-Fi

  • Website buttons. When you add a button for a website, the iPhone fetches that site's name and icon from that site, like a browser would.
  • Album artwork. When Media shows a song, the Mac may download its artwork from the music service that is playing it.
  • Install and renewal. The install command downloads Glasspad's source code from GitHub. Building and signing it uses Apple's Xcode and your Apple ID, under Apple's own terms.

Permissions

The Mac app asks for Accessibility, so it can move the pointer, type and arrange windows when you ask from the iPhone. It may ask for Automation, so it can show and control Spotify or Music. The iPhone app asks for Local Network, to find your Mac. You can turn any of these off in Settings.

This website

This site uses no cookies, no analytics and no trackers. It is hosted on Vercel, which keeps standard server logs, such as IP addresses, as any web host does. The demo film is a YouTube video in privacy-enhanced mode, and it loads only when you press play.
