Glasspad vs Stream Deck Mobile

Stream Deck Mobile, from Elgato, turns an iPhone or iPad into Stream Deck keys for a Mac or Windows PC running the Stream Deck app, with hundreds of plugins for streaming and creative tools. It is free for 6 keys; Pro gives up to 64 and costs $2.99 a month, $24.99 a year or $49.99 once. Glasspad is a free, open source iPhone app for the Mac only, with no key limit and buttons that change with the app in front, plus a trackpad, keyboard, remote and window control. For live streaming, Stream Deck's plugins make it the stronger choice.

Which one fits

Choose Stream Deck Mobile if

  • You stream, and want OBS scenes, Twitch and other plugins.
  • You already use a Stream Deck and want the same profiles on your phone.
  • You use Windows, an iPad or Android.

Choose Glasspad if

  • You want a free deck with no key limit for everyday Mac work.
  • You want buttons that change with the app in front, ready made.
  • You want the same iPhone to be a trackpad, keyboard and remote too.

Questions

Is Glasspad a free alternative to Stream Deck Mobile?
For everyday Mac work, yes: apps, websites, shortcuts, macros and Mac controls, with no key limit. It has no streaming plugins.
How many keys does Stream Deck Mobile give for free?
Six, with unlimited actions. Pro raises it to 64.
Can I use both?
Yes. They do not get in each other's way.
Does Glasspad work on iPad?
No. Glasspad is made for iPhone.

Install

Glasspad is free and open source (MIT). It needs a Mac on macOS 14 or later and an iPhone on iOS 18 or later, on the same Wi-Fi. To install, you also need Xcode 26 or later (free on the App Store), a cable for the first install, and a free Apple ID. Paste this into Terminal on your Mac:

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Soham109/glasspad/main/install.sh | bash

It waits for you where Apple needs a click. With a free Apple ID, apps you install yourself stop opening after 7 days; your Mac renews Glasspad over Wi-Fi before then. Every step, with pictures: Install. Help: Guide.
