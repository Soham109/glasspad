Who makes Glasspad

I am Soham Aggarwal. I build Glasspad myself and publish all of its code on GitHub under the MIT license, so anyone can read it, build it and change it.

I made it because I wanted my iPhone to do the jobs of a trackpad, a remote and a button deck for my Mac, without an account, a subscription or a cloud service in between.
