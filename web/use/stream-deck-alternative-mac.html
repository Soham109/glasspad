A free Stream Deck alternative for Mac

Glasspad turns your iPhone into a deck of buttons for your Mac, free. Its Actions mode shows buttons for the app in front, ready made for more than 70 apps, and below them your own: apps, websites, keyboard shortcuts, recorded macros, shortcuts from the Shortcuts app, and Mac controls like Do Not Disturb, Night Shift, Keep Awake and Split Screen. There are no key limits, ads or subscriptions, and it is open source (MIT). It has no physical keys and no plugins for tools like OBS, so for live streaming a Stream Deck is still the better fit.

How Glasspad does it

Actions

  • The top follows the app in front on your Mac. More than 70 apps have their own buttons.
  • Pinned holds yours. Folders keep them tidy.

Library

  • Every app, Mac control, macro, shortcut and website, with search.
  • Record steps on your Mac once, then replay them with a tap.

Any button

  • Touch and hold a button to change its name, symbol, color and size.
  • Add a website and its logo appears by itself.

Questions

Is it really free?
Yes. Every feature, with no limit on pinned buttons, no ads and no subscription. The code is under the MIT license.
Can it run my Shortcuts?
Yes. Any shortcut from the Shortcuts app on your Mac can be a button.
Can a button press a keyboard shortcut?
Yes. Add a Keyboard Shortcut button and set the keys, such as command C, or a hotkey you set in another app.
Does it work with OBS or Twitch?
Only through keyboard shortcuts you set up in those apps. Glasspad has no streaming plugins; Stream Deck has many.
Does it work with Windows or Android?
No. Glasspad is for an iPhone and a Mac only.

Install

Glasspad is free and open source (MIT). It needs a Mac on macOS 14 or later and an iPhone on iOS 18 or later, on the same Wi-Fi. To install, you also need Xcode 26 or later (free on the App Store), a cable for the first install, and a free Apple ID. Paste this into Terminal on your Mac:

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Soham109/glasspad/main/install.sh | bash

It waits for you where Apple needs a click. With a free Apple ID, apps you install yourself stop opening after 7 days; your Mac renews Glasspad over Wi-Fi before then. Every step, with pictures: Install. Help: Guide.
