Control your Mac's displays and windows from your iPhone

Glasspad's Displays mode shows your Mac's displays on your iPhone the way System Settings draws them, with every window on them. Tap a window to bring it forward, drag it to another display, or touch and hold it to tile, split, enter full screen, minimize or close it. Its settings set brightness, resolution, Night Shift, the main display, mirroring and the arrangement, and a new resolution goes back after 15 seconds unless you keep it.

How Glasspad does it

Displays

  • Your displays as in System Settings, with every window on them.
  • Tap a window to bring it forward, or drag it to another display.
  • Touch and hold a window for the halves, Fill, Center, Split With, full screen, minimize and close.
  • The settings button sets brightness, resolution, Night Shift, the main display, mirroring and the arrangement.

Two windows side by side in one tap: split screen from your iPhone.

Questions

Can I move a window to another monitor from my iPhone?
Yes. Drag it onto the other display in Displays, or tap a Next Display button in Actions.
Can I change the resolution from my iPhone?
Yes, in the settings in Displays. A new resolution or mirroring goes back after 15 seconds unless you keep it, so a blank screen fixes itself.
Does it work with one display?
Yes. You still get the list of windows, and tiling, brightness, resolution and Night Shift.
What does the Mac need to allow?
Accessibility, in System Settings › Privacy & Security, so Glasspad can move windows. The installer walks you through it.

Install

Glasspad is free and open source (MIT). It needs a Mac on macOS 14 or later and an iPhone on iOS 18 or later, on the same Wi-Fi. To install, you also need Xcode 26 or later (free on the App Store), a cable for the first install, and a free Apple ID. Paste this into Terminal on your Mac:

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Soham109/glasspad/main/install.sh | bash

It waits for you where Apple needs a click. With a free Apple ID, apps you install yourself stop opening after 7 days; your Mac renews Glasspad over Wi-Fi before then. Every step, with pictures: Install. Help: Guide.
