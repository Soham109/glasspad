Use your iPhone as a Mac keyboard

To type on your Mac from your iPhone, you need an app on both. Glasspad is a free, open source one: in its Keyboard mode, what you type or dictate on the iPhone lands wherever the cursor is on your Mac, over Wi-Fi. It has control, option, command and shift, the usual shortcuts, arrows, and F1 to F12, and it works with any Mac keyboard layout.

Install Glasspad Keyboard in the guide

How Glasspad does it

Keyboard

  • Type or dictate. The text lands wherever the cursor is on the Mac.
  • control, option, command and shift, the usual shortcuts, arrows, and fn for F1 to F12.
  • Hold a key to repeat it.
  • The Type layout puts the keyboard beside the trackpad on one screen.

Clipboard

  • Send to Mac: tap Paste to send what you copied on the iPhone, text or an image.
  • Get from Mac: tap it to copy what your Mac has copied.

Questions

Can I dictate on my Mac with my iPhone?
Yes. In Keyboard, tap the microphone on the iPhone keyboard and speak. The words are typed on the Mac.
Does it work with my keyboard layout?
Yes. Keys are sent for the Mac's own keyboard layout, whichever it is.
Can I send a long text from my iPhone?
Copy it on the iPhone, open Clipboard and tap Paste under Send to Mac. It is then on the Mac's clipboard, ready to paste.
Is what I type sent over the internet?
No. Glasspad pairs once with a QR code, encrypts everything, and your iPhone and Mac talk only over your Wi-Fi. There is no account.

Install

Glasspad is free and open source (MIT). It needs a Mac on macOS 14 or later and an iPhone on iOS 18 or later, on the same Wi-Fi. To install, you also need Xcode 26 or later (free on the App Store), a cable for the first install, and a free Apple ID. Paste this into Terminal on your Mac:

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Soham109/glasspad/main/install.sh | bash

It waits for you where Apple needs a click. With a free Apple ID, apps you install yourself stop opening after 7 days; your Mac renews Glasspad over Wi-Fi before then. Every step, with pictures: Install. Help: Guide.
