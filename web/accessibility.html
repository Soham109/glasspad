How accessible Glasspad is

What Glasspad supports today, what it does not yet, and how to tell me what to fix. Last updated 17 October 2026.

In the apps

SettingSupport
VoiceOverControls have labels and hints, with extra actions such as Edit on a button and skip on Remote's pad. Pairing and connection changes are spoken. The trackpad, Remote and Present pads still take your touches while VoiceOver is on.
Reduce MotionThe mode menu, pairing, Remote, Media, StandBy and the Mac's pairing code and window outline move less or not at all.
Larger textText follows your text size. Dense controls, such as keys and button grids, stop growing at a set size so they still fit on screen.
ContrastAutomated tests check every theme, light and dark, for text contrast of at least 4.5 to 1 (WCAG AA). The High Contrast theme is held to 7 to 1 (AAA).
Increase ContrastSome controls draw stronger outlines when it is on. For more, pick the High Contrast theme.
HapticsOptional. Turn them off or change their strength in the iPhone's Settings.

On this website

NeedSupport
KeyboardEvery link, menu and button works from the keyboard, with a visible focus ring and a Skip to content link.
Screen readersPages use headings, landmarks and labeled menus. Screenshots have text descriptions.
Reduce MotionAnimations stop or shorten when your device asks for less motion.
ContrastBody text is near black on off white. Gray text is at least 4.6 to 1 (WCAG AA).
Dark modeThe site is light only for now.

Not there yet

  • English only

    The apps and the site are in English.

  • Not yet tested

    Voice Control, Switch Control, and full keyboard use of the Mac app.

  • No outside audit

    Everything above is checked by me, not by an independent auditor.

Something in the way?

Tell me what you were trying to do and what stopped you: hello@sohamaggarwal.com or a GitHub issue. Accessibility problems are treated as bugs.
