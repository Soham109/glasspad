# Changelog

All notable changes are listed here. The format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com), and the project follows [semantic versioning](https://semver.org).

## [0.1.0] unreleased

### Added
- iPhone trackpad with a dot under each finger, Paper and Graphite themes.
- Pointer, tap and force click, drag, scroll with momentum, pinch, rotate, smart zoom, and swipes for Spaces, Mission Control, App Exposé, Launchpad and Show Desktop.
- Gestures follow the Mac's own trackpad settings, read live.
- Haptics for click, force click, detents and snaps.
- Modes: Dock, Keys, Media, Deck, Macros, Clipboard and Present.
- QR pairing, encrypted and authenticated transport, replay protection, revoking phones.
- Menu bar Mac app with permissions checklist and native or fallback gesture status.
- Phone safety: idle dimming, heat backoff, no brightness changes.
- App icons (Paper and Graphite), menu bar icon.
